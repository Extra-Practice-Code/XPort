Eva Barto

artist

Biography

Eva Barto graduated from the School of Fine Arts (Paris, 2013) and attended the post-graduate program at Ensba (Lyon, 2014). Recent solo shows include La BF15 (Lyon, 2015), Primo Piano (Paris, 2015), gb agency (Paris, 2016), Villa Arson (Nice, 2016) and soon Kunstverein Freiburg (April 2019). Her work have been presented at Centre Pompidou (Paris, 2016), Biennale de Rennes (2016), Kadist (Paris, 2016), Van Gelder (Amsterdam, 2016), Marcelle Alix (Paris, 2015), Fondation d’Entreprise Ricard (Paris, 2017) Wattis Art Center (San Francisco, 2017) and Secession (Vienna, 2018). In 2016 she initiated the publishing project Buttonwood Press. evabarto.net
